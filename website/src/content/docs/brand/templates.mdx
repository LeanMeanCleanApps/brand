## Reserved hues

Three hues are held for templates that might come next. Their marks are generated so the slots are visible, but nothing uses them yet. Think of them as chairs at the table for guests who haven't RSVP'd.

| Possible template | Hue |
| --- | --- |
| gRPC | Cyan |
| Worker | Red |
| CLI | Lime |

The hues are spread around the colour wheel so no two templates sit close together. Red is used for workers rather than rose, because rose sits too close to GraphQL's pink once it's shaded.

## Adding a template

1. Pick a hue that's well away from the ones in use. The reserved hues are the obvious first choices.
2. Add the template to `templates` in `scripts/brand.mjs`.
3. Run `npm run build`. Its logos, social preview, tokens and Starlight theme all appear.
