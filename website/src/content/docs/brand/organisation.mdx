The organisation has three versions of the mark. They share the shape with the templates and differ only in colour.

<MarkGrid which="org" />

## Slate, the default

Slate uses no template colour at all, just cool greys on ink. That lets it sit quietly next to five colourful templates without competing with them, and it works on any background.

Use slate for the organisation's avatar on GitHub, NuGet.org and Docker Hub, and anywhere the organisation appears next to its templates.

## Leaf, the alternative

Leaf is the green the earlier Clean templates and their documentation used. It's also the accent colour for organisation-level websites, like this one. Use it when you want the organisation mark to feel warmer, or to match a page that uses the leaf accent.

## Spectrum, for big moments

Spectrum carries all five template hues, arranged as in the original full-colour master and shaded with the same light-left rule as everything else. It's loud on purpose.

Use spectrum where colour is the point: the organisation profile banner, the link preview image, the hero of an organisation website, and stickers. Don't use it as an avatar, because at small sizes the colours turn to confetti.

## Why slate is the default

Once every template has its own hue, the organisation mark has one job: to say "these all belong together" without adding a sixth colour to the mix. Slate does that. It's also the version that holds up best at 32 pixels, which is where avatars spend most of their lives.
