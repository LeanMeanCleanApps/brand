The organisation has three versions of the mark. They share the shape with the templates and differ only in colour.

<MarkGrid which="org" />

## Spectrum, the default

Spectrum carries all five template hues, arranged as in the original full-colour master and shaded with the same light-left rule as everything else. It's loud on purpose.

Use spectrum everywhere the organisation shows up: the avatar on GitHub, NuGet.org and Docker Hub, favicons, the organisation profile banner, link previews, the hero of an organisation website, and stickers. At 48 pixels and below, use the small version, which keeps the core and two rings so it still reads as a ring of colour.

## Slate, the quiet one

Slate uses no template colour at all, just cool greys on ink. Use it when spectrum would be too loud: a slide crowded with template marks, a sponsor wall of other logos, or anywhere a calm, neutral version reads better.

## Leaf, the alternative

Leaf is the green the earlier Clean templates and their documentation used. It's also the accent colour for organisation-level websites, like this one, because a mark with five colours can't be a link colour. Use the leaf mark when you want the organisation to feel warmer, or to match a page that uses the leaf accent.

## Why spectrum is the default

The organisation mark has one job: to say "these all belong together". Spectrum does that literally, because it holds every template's hue. Next to a template's icon on NuGet.org, it reads as the family that template belongs to. It's also the version people remember.
