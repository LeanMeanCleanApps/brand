## Neutrals

| Name | Hex | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0B132A` | Backgrounds, tiles, the core on light backgrounds |
| Paper | `#FBFAF8` | Text on ink, the core on dark backgrounds, light page backgrounds |

## Organisation colours

Slate is the organisation mark's colour. Leaf is the accent for organisation-level websites and the alternative organisation mark.

## Shades in the mark

Each ring takes a fixed shade from its scale. The left half uses the first shade and the right half the next one down.

| Ring | Dark backgrounds | Slate on dark | Light backgrounds | Slate on light |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ring 1 | 300 / 400 | 200 / 300 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 |
| Ring 2 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 | 700 / 800 | 600 / 700 |
| Ring 3 | 400 / 500 | 300 / 400 | 600 / 700 | 500 / 600 |
| Ring 4 | 300 / 400 | 200 / 300 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 |
| Ring 5 | 500 / 600 | 400 / 500 | 700 / 800 | 600 / 700 |

The core is paper on dark backgrounds and ink on light ones.

## Colour around the mark

- Body text on ink is paper, and secondary text is slate 300.
- On ink, a template's 300 shade is its accent: kickers, command chips and highlights on its social preview.
- On a docs site, a template's 400 shade is the accent in dark mode and its 700 shade in light mode. The [Starlight themes](../../kit/tokens/) set this up for you.
