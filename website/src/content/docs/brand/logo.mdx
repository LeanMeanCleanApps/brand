Every Lean Mean Clean Apps template uses the same mark: a solid core inside five rings, cut into segments by straight gaps. The shape never changes. Colour is the only thing that tells one template from another.

## Why rings

Clean Architecture is usually drawn as circles inside circles, with every dependency pointing inwards. Our templates work the same way. The Application and Infrastructure layers are identical in every repository, and only the presentation layer changes.

So the core is the same paper white in every logo, and the rings around it change colour from template to template. Any resemblance to a certain billionaire's chest piece is a coincidence we're happy to live with.

## Anatomy

The mark is drawn on a 1024 unit square, centred on 512. Every gap is 20 units wide and straight-sided, so a cut is the same width at the inner edge as at the outer edge.

| Part | Radius (units) | Segments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | 0 to 104 | 1 |
| Ring 1 | 130 to 200 | 8, cut every 45° |
| Ring 2 | 220 to 274 | 6, cut on the diagonals and the vertical axis |
| Ring 3 | 294 to 364 | 8, cut every 45° |
| Ring 4 | 384 to 424 | 8, cut every 45° |
| Ring 5 | 444 to 480 | 12, cut every 30° |

The geometry lives in `scripts/geometry.mjs`. Change it there and rebuild, never by hand in a single file.

## How it's coloured

Three rules cover every mark in the library:

1. **One hue per mark.** A template uses only its own hue. The organisation marks use slate, leaf, or the full spectrum.
2. **Rings alternate.** Neighbouring rings use different shades, so the rings stay distinct without relying on the gaps alone.
3. **Light on the left.** The left half of every ring is one shade lighter than the right half. Every change of colour lands on a gap, never in the middle of a segment.

The exact shades are on the [Colour](../colour/) page.

## The small version

Below about 48 pixels the five rings blur together. The small version keeps the core and two rings with much wider cuts, so it still reads as the mark at favicon size.

## Backgrounds

The standard mark is made for dark backgrounds, and it looks best on ink. On light backgrounds, either use the `-light` version, which moves every ring three shades darker and swaps the core to ink, or keep the ink tile or circle behind the standard mark.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Leave clear space of at least an eighth of the mark's width on every side. The tile, square and circle versions already include it.
- Don't use the full mark below 64 pixels, or the small mark below 16 pixels.

## Please don't

- Add glows, gradients, shadows or outlines.
- Rotate it. The light left half and the vertical cut through the second ring only work the right way up.
- Mix two templates' hues in one mark, or recolour single segments.
- Put text inside it or stretch it.
- Redraw it. Every file comes from the generator, so fix the generator instead.
